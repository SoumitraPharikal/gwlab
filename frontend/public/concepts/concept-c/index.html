The Four Regimes of Compact Coalescence

REGIME 01 · POST-NEWTONIAN
Early Inspiral
20 Hz ≤ f ≤ 45 Hz · v/c < 0.2
Point-mass orbital decay driven by leading quadrupole emission. The analytic TaylorF2 phase matches post-Newtonian series up to 3.5PN order with stationary-phase integration.
REGIME 02 · STRONG FIELD
Late Inspiral
45 Hz ≤ f ≤ 67.6 Hz · v/c ∼ 0.3
Higher-order relativistic corrections become dominant. The symmetric mass ratio \(\eta\) strongly influences the rate of orbital chirp acceleration as the bodies approach ISCO.
REGIME 03 · NON-LINEAR
Plunge & Merger
DYNAMICAL TRANSITION · NUMERICAL RELATIVITY
The post-Newtonian series breaks down as the binary transitions to plunge. While \(r=6M\) marks the test-particle Schwarzschild ISCO, comparable-mass binaries experience strong mutual dynamical spacetime deformations; \(r=6M\) is not a universal merger boundary. Merger is modeled by Numerical Relativity (outside TaylorF2 scope).
REGIME 04 · PERTURBATION
Quasi-Normal Ringdown
f_QNM ∼ 250 Hz · τ ∼ 4 ms · PERTURBATIVE
The distorted remnant Kerr black hole sheds its non-axisymmetric multipole moments through exponentially damped quasi-normal gravitational modes (Teukolsky perturbation theory; not modeled by TaylorF2).
