Phase 0 Design Review
DIRECTION A SCHOLARLY JOURNAL

Concept A: Scientific Journal

Living Reviews in Relativity · Scholarly Plates

Asymmetric 2-column editorial grid inspired by classical scientific treatises. Folio headers, formal abstract boxes, academic plate figures with engraved hairline borders, and academic sidenotes for technical qualifications. Warm cream and Venetian oxblood accents.

DIRECTION B CALTECH / LIGO LAB

Concept B: Instrument Laboratory

Caltech / Hanford Control Room Rack Console

High-density telemetry racks, modular aluminum-brushed bays, illuminated CRT phosphor vector traces with reticle grids, stepped potentiometers, status LEDs, and diagnostic buses. Built for high-frequency scientific investigation and direct signal monitoring.

Open Hero (Index) → Open Waveforms Analyzer →
DIRECTION C CONTEMPORARY MONOGRAPH

Concept C: Contemporary Physics Atlas

Geometric Gravitation · Multi-Scale Atlas

Expansive geometric canvas featuring celestial coordinate grids, radiating quadrupole wavefront spirals, and a multi-scale decomposition across the four coalescence regimes. Blends modern geometric gravitation monograph elegance with interactive parameter exploration.
